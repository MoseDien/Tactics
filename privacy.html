Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

iTactics is a chess tactics training app for iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

iTactics does not collect, transmit, or sell personal information. The app does not require an account, email address, name, or phone number.

Data stored on your device

Your local rating, puzzle progress, batch history, and app preferences are stored locally on your device using Apple system storage. This data is used only to provide the app's training and review features.

Network access and third parties

iTactics does not use advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or third-party data services. The bundled puzzle library is available offline and the app does not send puzzle activity to a server.

Children's privacy

Because iTactics does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children or adults.

Data deletion

You can remove locally stored app data by deleting iTactics from your device. Reinstalling the app starts with a clean local profile.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated with a new revision date.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the developer through the support contact listed on the App Store product page.
